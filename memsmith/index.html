About

MemSmith is a calm daily brain workout: three short memory games — visual tracking, sequence recall, and hidden pairs — then a streak for finishing today’s set. Miss a day? A streak freeze can save your run. When you’re done, unlimited free practice is unlocked.

Designed to be clear, not noisy. No accounts, no ads, no subscriptions — your progress stays on your device. Optional reminders help you come back tomorrow.

Open the app. Finish three rounds. Beat yourself next time.
