Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 18, 2026

MemSmith (“the App”) is provided by Altered Edges, LLC (“we”, “us”, “our”), a United States company. This policy describes how we collect, use, share, and protect information when you use the App. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

No accounts

MemSmith does not require an account and does not ask you to provide a name, email address, or other contact details inside the App. Your streak, freeze token, tiers, scores, and reminder preference are stored locally on your device.

Information we collect

  • Analytics: we use Firebase Analytics (Google) to understand aggregate app usage (for example app opens, tutorial and daily round events). This may include device and app identifiers, approximate location derived from IP, and usage events. Analytics data is not used by us to identify you by name.
  • Local notifications (optional): if you opt in, the App schedules local reminders on your device. Notification preference is stored locally. We do not send remote push notifications in v1.
  • Technical data: crash and diagnostic information may be processed automatically as typical for mobile apps and third-party SDKs.

The App does not include advertising SDKs (no AdMob) in v1.

How we use information

We use the information above to:

  • Provide, operate, maintain, and improve the App
  • Understand feature usage and soft-launch funnel health
  • Comply with law and respond to lawful requests

Service providers

We use Google services, including Firebase Analytics. Google processes information as described in Firebase Privacy information and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Your choices

You may decline reminder opt-in or disable notifications in iOS Settings. Uninstalling the App removes local progress stored on your device. For questions about this policy, email alterededges@gmail.com.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the revised policy on this page and update the “Last updated” date.
