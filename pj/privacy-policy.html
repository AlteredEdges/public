Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 26, 2026

Pocket Judge (“the App”) is provided by Altered Edges, LLC (“we”, “us”, “our”), a United States company. This policy describes how we collect, use, share, and protect information when you use the App or related services. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

Information we collect

  • Account and profile information: such as email address, display name, and other profile details you provide when you register or update your account.
  • Sign-in providers: if you choose Sign in with Google or Sign in with Apple, authentication is processed by Google or Apple under their respective terms and privacy policies. We receive information they make available to us (for example an email address or user identifier) as part of creating or linking your account.
  • Invitation data: invitation codes and related records used to operate invite-only sign-up and referral features.
  • Contest and community content: text, images, and other content you submit in connection with contests (including entries, captions, titles, rules, and messages visible in the App), plus related metadata needed to operate schedules, roles (such as creator, participant, judge), scoring, and results.
  • Push notifications: if you opt in, we use platform push services (for example Firebase Cloud Messaging on supported devices) to deliver notifications. That can involve a device token or similar identifier processed by Google and by Apple or Google on your device platform. You can turn off notifications in your device or App settings where available.
  • Security and integrity: we use Firebase App Check and similar mechanisms to reduce abuse; those services may collect signals about the integrity of the app or device environment.
  • Technical and operational data: information processed automatically when you use the App, such as IP address, device or app identifiers, logs, diagnostics, and timestamps, as typical for mobile apps and cloud backends. We may also load fonts or other assets from third-party networks where our software relies on them.

How we use information

We use the information above to:

  • Provide, operate, maintain, and improve the App
  • Authenticate users, enforce invite-only access, and secure accounts
  • Run contests, judging, galleries, and results according to contest settings
  • Send notifications and other communications you have opted into (for example push notifications)
  • Detect, prevent, and respond to fraud, abuse, and security incidents
  • Comply with law, enforce our terms, and respond to lawful requests
  • Maintain logs, backups, and internal diagnostics needed to run the service reliably

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area or United Kingdom), we rely on appropriate legal bases such as performing our contract with you, legitimate interests that are not overridden by your rights, compliance with legal obligations, and consent where we ask for it.

Who can see contest-related content

Visibility depends on each contest’s settings and your role. Other users who are part of the same contest (for example the creator, participants, and judges) may see submissions, scores, or results as the App is designed to show them. Public contests may expose certain information more broadly than private ones. Do not submit content you are not comfortable sharing under those rules.
