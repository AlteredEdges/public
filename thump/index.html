Thump

Minimal metronome for guitar practice

About

Thump is a minimal metronome built for guitar practice on a music stand: large BPM display, thumb-friendly play/stop, full-width visual pulse, and a dark UI that stays readable under stage lights. Portrait and landscape. No accounts.
