Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 18, 2026

Thump Metronome (“the App”) is provided by Altered Edges, LLC (“we”, “us”, “our”), a United States company. This policy describes how we collect, use, share, and protect information when you use the App. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

No accounts

Thump does not require an account and does not ask you to provide a name, email address, or other contact details inside the App. Your preferred BPM and similar settings are stored locally on your device.

Information we collect

  • Analytics: we use Firebase Analytics (Google) to understand aggregate app usage (for example app opens and metronome start / stop / BPM change events). This may include device and app identifiers, approximate location derived from IP, and usage events. Analytics data is not used by us to identify you by name.
  • Advertising: we show banner ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier), IP address, and other data described in Google’s policies to serve and measure ads. You can limit ad tracking in your device settings.
  • Technical data: crash and diagnostic information may be processed automatically as typical for mobile apps and third-party SDKs.

How we use information

We use the information above to:

  • Provide, operate, maintain, and improve the App
  • Understand feature usage and stability
  • Show advertising that supports a free App
  • Comply with law and respond to lawful requests

Service providers

We use Google services, including Firebase Analytics and Google AdMob. Google processes information as described in Firebase Privacy information and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Your choices

You may reset or limit the advertising identifier in iOS Settings. Uninstalling the App removes local BPM settings stored on your device. For questions about this policy, email alterededges@gmail.com.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the revised policy on this page and update the “Last updated” date.
