Account deletion for Word Slug 2

Altered Edges, LLC is the developer of Word Slug 2. This page explains how to request that your game account and associated data be deleted.

How to request account deletion

To delete your Word Slug 2 account, email alterededges@gmail.com with the subject line Account Deletion Request.

In your message, include the username or sign-in identifier you use in Word Slug 2, and optionally a brief reason for your request. We will process verified requests as soon as we can.

Other questions

For general help, contact us at alterededges@gmail.com.
